Where to Play Badminton in Australia

Looking for somewhere to play badminton in Australia? Use the map below to find courts, clubs and stadiums near you - it's the same map data Google Search uses, so it stays up to date as new venues open.

Tips for finding a place to play

  • Search the map above for "badminton" near your suburb or town to see nearby courts, clubs and community centres.
  • Most Australian states and territories have their own badminton association that lists affiliated clubs, competitions and casual sessions - a quick search for "[your state] badminton association" is a good starting point.
  • Local community and leisure centres often hire out multi-purpose courts by the hour, even if they aren't dedicated badminton venues.
  • Universities and PCYC/YMCA centres frequently run casual or social badminton nights that are open to the public.
